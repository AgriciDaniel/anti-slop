# New AI classifier for indicating AI-written text

## What It Says

- OpenAI's own classifier identified 26 percent of AI-written text as likely AI-written
- It incorrectly labelled human-written text as AI-written 9 percent of the time
- OpenAI withdrew the classifier on 2023-07-20 due to its low rate of accuracy
