# Wikipedia Writing articles with large language models RfC close

## What It Says

- The RfC closed 2026-03-20 by a 44 to 2 margin under SNOW
- It prohibits using LLMs to generate or rewrite article content
- Two exceptions survive: copyediting your own writing, and LLM-assisted translation, both under mandatory human review
- It is a content guideline, not a policy
