# Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Produ

## What It Says

- Allowing AI increased task completion time by 19 percent for experienced developers on mature repositories
- Developers forecast a 24 percent speedup and still estimated a 20 percent speedup after the fact
- Economists forecast a 39 percent speedup and machine learning experts 38 percent
