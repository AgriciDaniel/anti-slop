# The Range Shrinks, the Threat Remains Re-evaluating LLM Package Hallucinations o

## What It Says

- 2026 frontier models hallucinate packages at 4.62 percent to 6.10 percent, an order of magnitude compression from the 2024 cohort spread
- Claude Haiku 4.5 measured 4.62 percent and GPT-5.4-mini measured 6.10 percent
- 53 hallucinated package names remained registerable at time of measurement
