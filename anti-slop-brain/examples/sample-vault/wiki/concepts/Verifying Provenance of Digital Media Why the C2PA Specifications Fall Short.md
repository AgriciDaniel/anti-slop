# Verifying Provenance of Digital Media Why the C2PA Specifications Fall Short

## What It Says

- The analysis concludes the C2PA specifications fail to achieve their claimed security goals
- It states the specifications should not yet be relied upon for high-stakes uses such as financial disclosures, journalism, or legal evidence
- It finds that specification version 2.4 does not address the identified problems
