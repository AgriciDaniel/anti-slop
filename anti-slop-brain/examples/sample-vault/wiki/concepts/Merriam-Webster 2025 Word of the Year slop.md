# Merriam-Webster 2025 Word of the Year slop

## What It Says

- Merriam-Webster defines slop as digital content of low quality that is produced usually in quantity by means of artificial intelligence
- Runners-up were gerrymander, performative, touch grass, six seven, and Lake Chargoggagoggmanchauggagoggchaubunagungamaugg
- The American Dialect Society also selected slop, voting 2026-01-09
