# EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations

## What It Says

- Regulation EU 2024/1689 applies from 2 August 2026, and Article 50(2) requires providers of covered generative systems to mark outputs in a machine-readable and detectable format, subject to the stated exceptions and technical-feasibility limits
- Regulation EU 2026/1744 gives systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 until 2 December 2026 to comply with Article 50(2)
- Article 99 permits fines for Article 50 non-compliance up to 15 million euro or 3 percent of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher, with the lower applicable cap controlling for SMEs and eligible small mid-cap enterprises
