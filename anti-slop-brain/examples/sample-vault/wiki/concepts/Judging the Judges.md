# Judging the Judges

## What It Says

- Judge style bias ranges from 0.10 to 0.76, with markdown preferred over plain text, against position bias of 0.04 or less
- Verbosity bias is model-family specific: Gemini and Llama plus 0.24 to plus 0.44, Claude minus 0.12, GPT-4o minus 0.04
