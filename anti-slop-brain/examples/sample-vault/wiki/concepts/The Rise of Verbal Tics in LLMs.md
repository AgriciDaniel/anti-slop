# The Rise of Verbal Tics in LLMs

## What It Says

- Verbal Tic Index across eight frontier models: Gemini 3.1 Pro 0.590 worst, GPT-5.4 0.411, Claude Opus 4.7 0.317, DeepSeek V3.2 0.295 best
- Human evaluation with n equal to 120 found a correlation of minus 0.87 between sycophancy and perceived naturalness
- Top tics include Absolutely, That's a great question, It's important to note, and delve
