# The Rise of Verbal Tics in LLMs

## What It Says

- The current arXiv abstract metadata and the rendered v3 paper disagree on the evaluated model roster
- The rendered v3 paper reports VTI values of 0.590 for Gemini 3.1 Pro, 0.411 for GPT-5.4, 0.317 for Claude Opus 4.7, and 0.295 for DeepSeek V3.2, but those rankings are blocked from operational use until the version discrepancy is resolved
- The rendered v3 paper reports a human evaluation with n equal to 120 and a correlation of minus 0.87 between sycophancy and perceived naturalness
