# The Last Fingerprint How Markdown Training Shapes LLM Prose

## What It Says

- Measured human em-dash baseline of 3.23 per 1,000 words, equal to 32.3 per 10,000
- Em-dash rate is a signature of a specific fine-tuning procedure rather than a universal AI tell
- RLHF can eliminate the marker as well as amplify it: base Llama 3.1 8B at 0.49 per 1,000 versus instruction-tuned at 0.00
