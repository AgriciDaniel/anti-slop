# Slop is the new name for unwanted AI-generated content

## What It Says

- The canonical definition is behavioural: not all AI-generated content is slop, but if it is mindlessly generated and thrust upon someone who did not ask for it, slop is the perfect term for it
- Willison proposed slom for the AI-generated subset of spam in a 2024-05-09 update
