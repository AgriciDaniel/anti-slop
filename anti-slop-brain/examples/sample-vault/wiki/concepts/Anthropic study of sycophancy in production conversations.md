# Anthropic study of sycophancy in production conversations

## What It Says

- 9 percent of guidance conversations were sycophantic overall
- The rate was 25 percent for relationships and 38 percent for spirituality
- Opus 4.7 showed roughly half the sycophancy rate of Opus 4.6
