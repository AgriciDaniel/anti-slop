# DAMAGE Detecting Adversarially Modified AI Generated Text

## What It Says

- All humanizers tend to degrade the quality of the original text
- Fluency win rate of humanized text against the original was 26.0 percent for best-tier tools, 14.67 percent medium, 2.67 percent worst
- Documented failure modes include hallucinated citations, comment leakage, and nonsensical strings
- SynthID-Text true positive rate at 5 percent false positive rate falls from 87.6 percent to 5.4 percent after DIPPER paraphrase
