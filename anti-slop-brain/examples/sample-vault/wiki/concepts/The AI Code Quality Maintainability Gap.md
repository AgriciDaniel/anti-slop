# The AI Code Quality Maintainability Gap

## What It Says

- Block duplication climbed from 40.3 per million changed lines in 2023 to 73.0 in 2026 year to date, a rise of 81 percent
- The 2026 report measures two-week code churn at plus 15 percent rather than reporting a churn level
