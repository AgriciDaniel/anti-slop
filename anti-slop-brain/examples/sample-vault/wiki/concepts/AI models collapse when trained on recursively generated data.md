# AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data

## What It Says

- Recursive training on generated data causes early collapse where distribution tails and variance are lost, and late collapse where the model converges to a low-variance point estimate
- The effect was demonstrated on Gaussian mixture models, variational autoencoders, and OPT language models
