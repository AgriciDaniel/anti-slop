# Overview

Anti-Slop Brain is a persistent operating brain for detection and repair of AI slop in prose, code, documentation, and agent output, grounded in corpus evidence rather than authorship detection.

![[brain-relationship-map.svg]]

## Buyer

Writers, engineers, and agent operators who ship AI-assisted work and need a defensible, source-cited way to find and fix substance defects without accusing anyone of using AI.
