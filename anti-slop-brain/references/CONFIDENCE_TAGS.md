# Confidence Tags

`evidence-based`: supported by controlled research or authoritative primary data.
`practitioner`: operationally useful applied judgment or field practice, not proven by studies.
`contested`: mixed, disputed, stale, or failed-replication evidence; use with a caveat.
`folklore`: popular claim without credible support; demoted, never presented as fact.

Ledger mapping: high -> evidence-based, med -> practitioner, low -> contested.

Usage rule: every domain claim carries exactly one lowercase confidence value and one matching tag: `#confidence/evidence-based`, `#confidence/practitioner`, `#confidence/contested`, or `#confidence/folklore`. Folklore is demoted, never asserted as truth. A bestseller or popularity is not evidence.
