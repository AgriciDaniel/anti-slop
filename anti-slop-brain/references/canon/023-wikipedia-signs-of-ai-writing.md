# 023. Wikipedia Signs of AI writing

Ledger: 023 | source: Wikipedia Signs of AI writing | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Wikipedia Signs of AI writing]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- The guide states the listed patterns are only potential signs of a problem, not the problem itself
- It warns explicitly not to merely treat the signs as the problems to be fixed, because that could just make detection harder
- It documents a markup taxonomy of vendor residue markers including oaicite, bracketed cite tokens, grok-card, and utm_source tracking parameters
- It documents a citations taxonomy covering fabricated references, invalid DOIs and ISBNs, and DOIs pointing at unrelated papers
- It maintains an ineffective-indicators list of signals that should not be treated as evidence
- It notes that AI vocabulary shifts by model era and gives dated cohorts
