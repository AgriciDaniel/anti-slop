# 018. Identifying Bias in Machine-generated Text Detection

Ledger: 018 | source: Identifying Bias in Machine-generated Text Detection | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Identifying Bias in Machine-generated Text Detection]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- English-language-learner essays are more likely to be flagged by machine-generated text detectors
- Non-White ELL students were disproportionately flagged relative to White ELL peers
- Human annotators showed no significant demographic bias on the same essays
