# 001. Measuring AI Slop in Text

Ledger: 001 | source: Measuring AI Slop in Text | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Measuring AI Slop in Text]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- LLM-as-judge agreement with human slop labels is near zero: GPT-5 kappa 0.01, DeepSeek-V3 kappa -0.01, o3-mini kappa 0.03
- Models flag slop at a 0.03 to 0.08 rate versus humans at 0.34, under-flagging by roughly five times
- Span-level slop extraction by GPT-5 runs at precision 0.14 and recall 0.11; a fine-tuned Qwen-7B reaches 0.32 and 0.30
