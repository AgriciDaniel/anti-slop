# 037. EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations

Ledger: 037 | source: EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations | confidence: practitioner | fold: [[EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- Article 50 transparency duties apply from 2026-08-02 and were not amended by the Digital Omnibus
- The Digital Omnibus, adopted by Parliament 2026-06-16 and Council 2026-06-29, deferred high-risk deadlines only
- Generative systems already on the market before 2026-08-02 have until 2026-12-02 for the Article 50(2) machine-readable marking requirement
- Penalties reach 15 million euro or 3 percent of turnover
