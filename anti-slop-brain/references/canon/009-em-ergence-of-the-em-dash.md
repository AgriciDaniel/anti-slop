# 009. Em-ergence of the em-dash

Ledger: 009 | source: Em-ergence of the em-dash | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Em-ergence of the em-dash]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- Em-dash prevalence in medRxiv Discussion sections rose from 4.23 percent pre-ChatGPT to 11.58 percent post, a 7.35 point rise with 95 percent CI 6.94 to 7.77 and odds ratio 2.96
- The trajectory runs at about 4 percent through 2023, 8.0 percent in 2024, and 20.3 percent in 2025
- A placebo split within the pre-LLM era showed no change, plus 0.13 points
- The author concludes the em-dash is a population-level indicator, not a per-paper detector of LLM use
