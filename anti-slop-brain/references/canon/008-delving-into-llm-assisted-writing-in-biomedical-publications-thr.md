# 008. Delving into LLM-assisted writing in biomedical publications through excess voca

## What It Says

- At least 13.5 percent of 2024 PubMed abstracts were processed with an LLM, reaching 40 percent in some subcorpora
- Excess words are overwhelmingly stylistic verbs and adjectives rather than content words
- The method is detector-free, comparing observed against extrapolated word frequencies
