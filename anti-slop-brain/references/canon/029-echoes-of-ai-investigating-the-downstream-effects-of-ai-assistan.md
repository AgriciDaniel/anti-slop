# 029. Echoes of AI Investigating the Downstream Effects of AI Assistants on Software M

Ledger: 029 | source: Echoes of AI Investigating the Downstream Effects of AI Assistants on Software M | confidence: practitioner | fold: [[Echoes of AI Investigating the Downstream Effects of AI Assistants on Software M]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- Phase 2 found no significant differences in subsequent code evolution, completion time, or quality between AI-assisted and unassisted development
- Phase 1, observational, found a 30.7 percent median reduction in completion time with an AI assistant, and an estimated 55.9 percent speedup for habitual AI users
- The study was preregistered with In-Principle Acceptance before data collection
