# 016. Voice Under Revision

Ledger: 016 | source: Voice Under Revision | confidence: practitioner | fold: [[Voice Under Revision]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- LLM revision decreases function words, contractions, and first-person pronouns while increasing vocabulary diversity and word length
- The shift persists even under explicit instructions to preserve the author's voice
- Rewritten texts converge in feature space regardless of their starting point
