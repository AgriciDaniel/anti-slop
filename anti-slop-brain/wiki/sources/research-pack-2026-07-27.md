# Research Pack 2026-07-27

Every source consulted for this brain, with its retrieval date and the tier
it was assigned. The pack exists so that a future reader can reconstruct what
was known on 2026-07-27 and can tell which claims have since expired.

Ledger sources are load bearing: a claim in the wiki must cite one of them.
Background sources informed the research but no wiki claim rests on them
alone. The split is deliberate. A source that only ever appears in a
bibliography is decoration, and decoration is the thing this brain exists to
remove.
