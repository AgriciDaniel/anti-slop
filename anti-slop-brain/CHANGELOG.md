# Changelog

All notable changes to the Anti-Slop Brain are recorded here. Dates are ISO
and versions are semantic.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-27

### Added

- Initial Anti-Slop Brain scaffold generated by Brainstein 1.2.0.
- Source ledger with 42 dated entries, 37 of them official or primary type,
  each carrying an evidence tier and explicit limitations.
- Three-layer architecture: deterministic scanners, structural procedures, and
  evidence-tiered soft signals that route to procedures rather than failing.
- The firewall: no authorship verdict, no hard fail on a stylistic marker,
  severity and confidence kept on separate axes, and no model self-gating.
- Wiki covering concepts, markers, procedures, surfaces, detection, evidence,
  and counterarguments.
- Ported wiki substance scorer adapted from the Claude Blog Brain.

### Known limitations

- Several 2026 citations are arXiv preprints verified at abstract level only
  and are tagged CONTESTED rather than EVIDENCE-BASED.
- Three sources could not be read behind auth walls and are recorded as open
  questions rather than omitted.
